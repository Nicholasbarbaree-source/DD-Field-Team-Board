Definitions & how this workbook is calculated
Notice Given
1st Date Fill-In Needed minus the date the case was opened.
Critical
48 hours (2 days) or less notice.
Last-Minute
Less than 7 days notice (but more than 48 hours).
Action Needed (KPI band & RM cards)
Fill-in cases still "New" with 14 or fewer days left until the 1st Fill-In date — the RM should already have movement on it (the 2-3 week rule). Performance cases don't have a comparable "still time left" tier — see Overdue below.
Overdue (KPI band & RM cards)
Combined across both case types: fill-in cases whose 1st Fill-In date has passed while still open/unresolved, plus performance cases that have gone more than 7 days with no action taken (still "New").
Fill-in actioning timeliness (chart)
Built from the case status-history log (every New→Sourcing/Driver Secured/etc. transition), not just current status. Deadlines: ≤14 days notice → RM should act within 1 day of the request; >14 days notice → RM should act with at least 14 days of runway left before the 1st Fill-In date; if the assignment itself spans more than 2 business weeks (Mon–Fri) and came in with >14 days notice, RM should act with 30 days of runway when possible. A case with zero action ever, past its deadline, counts as Late.
Avg Days to Close
Average days between a performance case opening and it reaching Closed or Service Completed, by the month it was opened. Needs at least 2 closed cases in a month to plot.
Chronic
Driver has 3 or more short-notice (Critical + Last-Minute) fill-in requests, all-time.
Data source
Fill-in figures come from the full case history (1,084 records, back to May 2025) plus a case status-history log (1,279 status changes covering 729 cases) for the timeliness chart. Performance figures come from the full performance-case history (260 records, back to June 2025), including closed and service-completed cases. This pull's performance export no longer carries a Status column, so an open case is one with no Closed Date and “overdue” means open more than 7 days; the In Review tier reads 0 until Status is added back to the report. Pulled 9/23/2026.
In Progress
Fill-in case that has moved off "New" but hasn't closed out — currently these are all at Driver Secured, meaning coverage is lined up but the assignment hasn't been completed yet.

Definitions & data source
New
Driver hasn't logged actual time yet — "No Time Reported" export, Status = New.
Driver Submitted
Driver logged time and it is queued for TaskUs (TU) first-pass review. This sits with the TaskUs team, not the Regional Manager, so it is displayed everywhere by status but is excluded from every RM flag, health colour and “on the RM” count on this board.
On the RM / RM-side
In Review (Initial Review Completed) plus Additional Information Required — the two statuses an RM can actually move. All flag thresholds, oldest-age and $-at-risk figures on the RM cards use this subset.
Additional Info Required
RM reviewed it and it's stuck pending more information — the log needing the most attention.
Approved
Closed out and paid — from the Extra Time Approved export.
Age
Days between the log's Date of Work and the report's as-of date. A proxy for how long an item has been sitting.
$ at risk
Sum of Total Paid Amount across Driver Submitted + Additional Info Required logs for that RM — not yet approved, not yet paid.
Initial Review Completed
RM has looked at it once but it's not yet fully approved — a mid-way status between Driver Submitted and Approved.
Open Expense
Driver-submitted expense (mileage, tolls, other) not yet Approved or Declined — from the Open Driver Expenses by Owner export.
Low margin
Approved daily log where Margin % (Margin $ ÷ Invoice Amount) is under 10% — the customer was invoiced little relative to what the driver was paid.
High margin
Approved daily log where Margin % is over 55% — invoiced well above what was paid out; worth checking pricing or driver pay for errors.
Flagged Approved Logs
Approved logs where the Driver Notes field is blank or under 15 characters (catches placeholders like "Monday" or "Nothing to add"), or where the RM's approval note is blank. Meant to catch logs approved without enough detail to show who approved the extra time, what order it was tied to, or where the driver was sent — not a full compliance check, just a thinness filter on the notes actually entered.
Data source
Everything inside the pay period comes from the 9/23/2026 morning pulls: Extra Time Approved 7:34 AM and Extra Time Reported 7:36 AM, both Date of Work 9/19–9/25, plus No Time Reported 7:34 AM, Date of Work 9/19–9/22. This is day 5 of 7 in the period, so approved and waiting counts are still mid-cycle. A separate wide Extra Time Approved pull (Date of Work 8/1–9/16) feeds only the 8/1–9/16 flagged-notes tab and the verifier breakdown — that one was not refreshed in this pull and still reflects 9/16. Pending Logs (Created Date 1/1–9/23, pulled 7:37 AM) supplies the backlog outside the pay period — this is why some open items show ages of 250+ days — plus in-period regular-hours logs, which the extra-time reports filter out and so can't speak to. Where Pending and the 7:36 AM extra-time pull describe the same log, the extra-time pull wins. Open Driver Expenses: as of 9/23/2026, 7:37 AM. Margin Issue Log: direct mirror of the Copy of Margin export, Date of Work > 1/1/2026, filtered to margin <10% or >55%, pulled 7:38 AM — every row links straight to its Daily Log record. Margin alerts (Approved logs only): Date of Work 1/1–9/23/2026, pulled 7:37 AM. All other reports pulled 9/23/2026, mid-morning.
Owning RM (inferred)
The margin-by-geo export carries no owner column, so each geo is matched to an RM by majority of the performance cases and daily logs in that geo over the last 90 days (falling back to full history where there is no recent activity). Confidence is that majority's share — under 70% means the geo is split or was recently reassigned.
Blended margin %
Total margin dollars divided by total invoiced across every log in the geo or account — not an average of per-log percentages, so a few extreme logs don't distort it.
